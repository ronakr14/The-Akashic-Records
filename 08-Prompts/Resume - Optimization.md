# AI Summary
Reusable prompt pack for optimizing a resume against a job description: extract JD requirements, tailor the CV with ATS-friendly language, rewrite experience bullets (action–task–result), build a role-fit matrix, run a brutal ATS audit, reframe for an industry pivot, and cut to one page — all without inventing experience. Merged from the former "Resume Optimization Prompt Pack".

---
Tailored CV, ATS-friendly bullets, role-fit scoring, pivots, one-page cuts.

## Extract from JD
> "Act as a recruiter. Analyze this job description and list the top skills, core responsibilities, hiring goals, must-use keywords, and what makes a standout candidate. Present it in a table. JD: [paste]"

## Tailor CV
> "Rewrite my CV for this job without inventing anything. Improve the summary, experience bullets, and skills section. Use ATS-friendly keywords from the JD, and make each bullet relevant and outcome-focused. CV: [paste] JD: [paste]"

## Upgrade bullets
> "Rewrite these CV bullets for this role using: accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z. Keep each bullet concise, specific, and aligned to the JD. If a metric is missing, add [ADD METRIC]. Role: [title] JD: [paste] Bullets: [paste]"

## Role-fit matrix
> "Create a role-fit matrix comparing my profile to this JD. Columns: requirement, my evidence, strength level, what to stress in my CV, what to address in the cover letter, and interview story to prepare. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]"

## Brutal ATS audit
> "Act as a strict ATS. Scan my resume [paste] against this JD [paste]. List every reason I get filtered before a human sees me: missing keywords, weak sections, bullets to fix, skills to move up, formatting issues. Then rewrite my summary to improve the match without keyword stuffing."

## Industry pivot
> "I'm moving from [industry A] to [industry B]. Rewrite my resume to reframe each experience as directly relevant to the target role. Make the pivot feel intentional, not accidental. Do not invent experience. Resume: [paste] Target role: [paste]"

## One-page cut
> "My resume is [X] pages. Cut it to one page without losing a single impressive achievement. Keep everything that matters, remove everything that doesn't, and say what you cut and why. Resume: [paste]"
