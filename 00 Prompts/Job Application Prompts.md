## Summary
Structured prompts for: resume tailoring, ATS optimization, interview preparation, LinkedIn branding.

## 1. Resume Optimization

### Extract from JD
> "Act as a recruiter. Analyze this job description and list the top skills, core responsibilities, hiring goals, must-use keywords, and what makes a standout candidate. Present it in a table. JD: [paste]"

### Tailor CV
> "Rewrite my CV for this job without inventing anything. Improve the summary, experience bullets, and skills section. Use ATS-friendly keywords from the JD, and make each bullet relevant and outcome-focused. CV: [paste] JD: [paste]"

### Upgrade Bullets
> "Rewrite these CV bullets for this role. Use action + task + result. Keep each bullet concise, specific, and aligned to the JD. If a metric is missing, add [ADD METRIC]. Role: [title] JD: [paste] Bullets: [paste]"

### Write Cover Letter
> "Write a tailored cover letter for this role using my background. Keep it clear, human, and specific. Mention 2–3 relevant achievements, connect them to the company's needs, and avoid clichés. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]"

### Role Fit Matrix
> "Create a role-fit matrix comparing my profile to this JD. Columns: requirement, my evidence, strength level, what to stress in my CV, what to address in the cover letter, and interview story to prepare. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]"

### ATS Score Improvement
> "Compare my CV to this JD and show missing keywords, weak sections, bullets to improve, skills to move up, and ATS issues. Then rewrite my summary to improve match without keyword stuffing. CV: [paste] JD: [paste]"

## 2. Interview Preparation

### Predict Interview Questions
> "Act as the hiring manager. Based on this JD and my background, generate 15 likely interview questions. Split them into technical, behavioral, and fit. For each, explain why it's asked and what a strong answer should include. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]"

### Build STAR Responses
> "Create 8 STAR interview answers from my background for this role. Cover leadership, problem-solving, teamwork, conflict, ownership, failure, achievement, and adaptability. Include a short interview-ready version too. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]"

### Full Application Pack
> "Create a full application pack for this job: CV summary, top skills, tailored cover letter, likely interview questions, smart questions for the interviewer, recruiter DM, and follow-up email. Use only my real experience. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]"

## 3. ATS System Prompts

### Resume Autopsy
> "Act as a brutal ATS system. Scan my resume [paste]. Tell me every reason I get filtered before a human ever sees me and what to fix immediately."

### Achievement Translator
> "Rewrite every bullet point on my resume [paste] using the formula: accomplished X measured by Y by doing Z. Make every line impossible to ignore."

### Keywords Injector
> "Analyze this job description [paste]. Extract every keyword the ATS is scanning for. Rewrite my resume [paste] to include every single one naturally."

### Gap Explainer
> "I have a [time period] gap in my resume. Write me three versions of how to address it — one for the resume, one for the cover letter, and one for the interview."

### Industry Pivot Builder
> "I am moving from [industry A] to [industry B]. Rewrite my resume to reframe every experience as directly relevant to my target role. Make the pivot feel intentional not accidental."

### One Page Cutter
> "My resume is [X] pages. Cut it to one page without losing a single impressive achievement. Keep everything that matters. Remove everything that does not."

### Cover Letter Closer
> "Write a cover letter for [role] at [company] using my resume [paste]. Hook in line one. Build genuine interest. Close with one line that makes them pick up the phone."

## 4. LinkedIn Branding

### Authority Content Ideas
> "Give me 15 LinkedIn post ideas that position me as an expert in [your niche] and attract inbound leads."

### Profile Strategy Audit
> "Act as a LinkedIn personal branding strategist. Audit my current profile and identify why it's not attracting inbound leads. Be brutally honest."

### Featured Section
> "Suggest what I should add to my Featured section to increase credibility and conversion."

### Experience Enhancement
> "Rewrite my work experience to focus on measurable results, transformations, and impact — not responsibilities."

### Inbound Message Script
> "Create a natural, non-salesy DM response framework for when prospects reach out after seeing your profile."

### About Transformation
> "Turn my 'About' section into a client-centered story that builds authority, shows proof, and ends with a strong call-to-action."

### Magnetic Headline
> "Rewrite my LinkedIn headline to clearly state who I help, the result I create, and why you're different — optimized for search + curiosity."
