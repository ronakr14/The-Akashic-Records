## 1. MCP Is Turning Into Infrastructure — Not Just an AI Integration Layer

**Best overall pick**

The MCP `2026-07-28` specification changed the protocol core to stateless request/response, enabling ordinary load balancing, cacheable tool discovery, header-based routing, redesigned long-running interactions, and stronger authorization. More importantly, the August 22 roadmap now explicitly focuses on agentic messaging, HTTP-native transport, enterprise identity/security, and better SDK primitives. ([Model Context Protocol Blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The 2026-07-28 Specification | Model Context Protocol Blog"))

### Working title

**MCP Is Becoming Infrastructure: What Data Engineers Need to Know**

### Why it's timely

We're past the “what is MCP?” phase.

The interesting engineering question is now:

> **How do you operate MCP as production infrastructure?**

That is much closer to your architecture interests than another MCP tutorial.

### Outline

2. Why the old stateful model didn't scale cleanly
