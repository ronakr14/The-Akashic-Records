Best use case:  
Developer-first transactional email platform for sending, testing, and managing application emails with modern APIs, React email support, and strong DX. Ideal for auth emails, notifications, onboarding flows, and AI app communication pipelines.

Alternative:

# resend

Best use case:  
Lightweight **email resend/automation tool** for transactional or marketing emails, ensuring delivery retries and tracking without complex infrastructure.

Alternative: — **Postal / Mailgun** — better when you need **scalable cloud email infrastructure with analytics and high deliverability**.
