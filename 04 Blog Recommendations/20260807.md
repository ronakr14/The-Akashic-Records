# 1. The Modern Data Engineer's New Responsibility: Building Context, Not Just Pipelines ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Why it's timely**

Across the industry, the conversation is shifting from "build better prompts" to "build better context." Enterprises are realizing that agentic AI succeeds or fails based on metadata, governance, semantics, and data quality—not prompt tricks. Recent research and industry commentary increasingly frame context engineering as a core engineering discipline. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09619?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
