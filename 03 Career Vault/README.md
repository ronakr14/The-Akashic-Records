# Career Vault

> Career-long compounding knowledge. Not project-specific — *how I think about engineering*.

## Purpose

This folder captures knowledge that outlives any single project:

- **ADRs** — Why decisions were made (not just what)
- **Open Questions** — Things I haven't resolved yet; tracked so they resurface
- **Lessons & Trade-offs** — Experience-derived; "if I could go back, I'd..."
- **Incidents & Postmortems** — Failure analyses; what broke and why

## When to add

| Trigger | Folder |
|---|---|
| Made an architectural decision with trade-offs | `ADRs/` |
| Realized you don't know something and it matters | `Open Questions/` |
| Learned a lesson the hard way | `Lessons & Trade-offs/` |
| Something broke in production or in a project | `Incidents & Postmortems/` |

## How to name

- ADRs: `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` (e.g., `2026-06-24-avoid-kafka-for-small-projects.md`)
- Open Questions: `Q-<slug>.md` (e.g., `Q-when-to-use-polars-vs-pandas.md`)
- Lessons: `LESSON-<slug>.md`
- Incidents: `INC-<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`
