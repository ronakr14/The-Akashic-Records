# Incidents & Postmortems

> Failure analyses. What broke, why, and what changed.

## Why track incidents?

- Failure is the fastest teacher
- Patterns across incidents reveal systemic blind spots
- Future-you will face similar failure modes

## Format

```markdown
# [Incident Title]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Severity:** High / Medium / Low
**Duration:** [How long it lasted]

## Timeline
- HH:MM — What happened
- HH:MM — Detection
- HH:MM — Mitigation
- HH:MM — Resolution

## Root Cause
What actually caused this?

## What Went Right
(There's always something)

## What Went Wrong
Where were the gaps?

## Action Items
- [ ] Preventive measure 1
- [ ] Detection measure 2

## See Also
- [[related-note]]
```
