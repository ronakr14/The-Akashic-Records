# Open Questions

> Unresolved questions that matter. Tracked so they resurface when I encounter new information.

## Why track open questions?

- They're the *edges* of my knowledge — what I don't know shapes my decisions as much as what I do
- Revisiting them over time shows intellectual growth
- Answering one often unlocks a chain of new insights

## Format

```markdown
# [Question]

**Status:** Open / Resolved
**Date Opened:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Domain:** [e.g., data engineering, distributed systems, career]

## What I know now
Current understanding / partial answer

## What's missing
What would resolve this?

## Context
Where did this question come from? What decision depends on it?

## See Also
- [[related-note]]
```
